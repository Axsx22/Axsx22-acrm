## Reporting policy

Stakeholder reporting summarizes shipped implementation, material risks, next steps, and decisions needed. It does not promote prototypes, historical artifacts, demonstrations, or conceptual research into implemented capability.

Claims in stakeholder updates must remain consistent with the repository's evidence policy and the current implementation status. The executive deck is a communication artifact; detailed contracts, tests, development-status records, and research artifacts remain the authoritative technical evidence.

## Update cadence

Create a dated stakeholder update when a reporting period closes or when a material milestone, risk, or decision changes. Prefer a paired Markdown + PowerPoint artifact using the naming convention:

`STAKEHOLDER_UPDATE_YYYY-MM-DD.md`  
`ACRM_Executive_Update_YYYY-MM-DD.pptx`
