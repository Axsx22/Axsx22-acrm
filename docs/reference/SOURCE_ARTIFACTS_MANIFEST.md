## Evidence rule

A document being present in this repository means that the artifact is preserved and traceable. It does **not** mean that every claim in the artifact has been empirically validated. Benchmark specifications, roadmaps, hypotheses, demos, unit tests, and independent benchmark results remain distinct evidence classes.
